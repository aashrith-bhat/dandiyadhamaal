The Celebration

Where tradition meets full volume

Dandiya Dhamaal, hosted by Rotaract Bangalore West, is not just an event — it is a sea of swirling chaniya cholis, mirrored kedis and dandiyas clashing in perfect rhythm. From first-timers to Garba champions, everyone becomes part of one giant circle under the October sky.

A sprawling open ground, a powerhouse line-up of live musicians, folk artists from Rajasthan and Gujarat, a photobooth to capture your twirls, and a hot dinner spread to send everyone home smiling.
